# Content ideas

A starting bank for suggestions. Mix and adapt these to the season, what's trending and what the person asks for.
Every idea follows the brand rules: no medical claims, no generated logos or packaging text, and English copy.

## Nael & Newk

**TikTok / Reels (9:16)**
1. *"My 9pm wind-down"*: a series of calm evening stills (tea, dim lamp, linen sheets) with a soft `sleep`
   music bed. The caption leads to Magnesium Bisglycinate.
2. *"What chelated means, in 10 seconds"*: a simple still-life backdrop with on-screen text added in CapCut,
   and the `calm` music bed.
3. *"Gentle on the stomach"*: a morning routine with a glass of water and breakfast light. The real packshot
   is composited on the table.
4. *Post-workout cool-down*: stretching on a sunny terrace, an `upbeat` music bed, and Vitamin D3 + K2 for
   exercise recovery support.
5. *"Sunshine vitamin for indoor workers"*: an office worker by a bright window during the Malaysian monsoon
   season, featuring D3 + K2.
6. *Perimenopause and sleep*: a calm woman in her late 40s in a soft bedroom morning, with gentle and
   respectful copy.
7. *Ramadan / Raya routine*: sahur and iftar table still-life with dates and water, featuring Halal (JAKIM).

**Instagram / Facebook / Threads (1:1, 4:5)**
- A quote card with "Wellness doesn't need to be complicated." on a linen texture backdrop.
- A product carousel: three real packshots on matching stone-table backdrops, one per slide.
- "Which one is for you?": a magnesium vs magnesium + zinc comparison card on a sand-coloured background.
- A customer-review card, using real reviews from the shop only.

**Shopify (banner, product-square)**
- A hero banner for each season (sunlit interior, with empty space on the left for text).
- Lifestyle product images: the real box composited onto a `product-backdrop` scene.

**Membership and promos**
- "Join our membership": a warm welcome banner with a gift-wrapped linen parcel and no text in the image.
- Payday sale, 11.11, 12.12, Raya and CNY promos: festive but still calm. Use soft accents, not red-gold
  overload. Add the text in an editor.
