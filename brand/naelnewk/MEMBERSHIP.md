## How it works (in customer words)

- **Join free** on the Rewards page (`naelnewk.com/pages/rewards`) by adding your mobile number. Only members
  earn and redeem points, and only orders paid after joining count.
- **Welcome gift: 300 points** when you join.
- **Earn 1 point for every RM1** spent on products, after discounts and excluding shipping and tax. Points are
  added once your order is paid. Guest checkouts don't earn points.
- **Redeem 100 points for RM5 off.** You get a personal voucher code, valid for 90 days. So the welcome
  points alone are worth RM15 off, as three vouchers.
- **Points expire 3 months** after they're earned. The oldest points are used first.
- **One voucher per order.** Vouchers can't be combined with other discounts, and orders that use a rewards
  voucher don't earn points.
- Refunds take back the points the order earned.

UI phrases already in use: "Rewards" (program name), "Join to earn these points.", "Redeem points",
"Your vouchers", "Points activity", "More ways to earn", "More rewards coming soon.", "How it works".

## Extras (built, check whether they're switched on before promoting)

- **Post about us on social media: 50 points.** "Share a post featuring our products on Instagram, TikTok or
  Facebook, then send us the link. We'll add your points once we've checked it." Up to 5 times per member,
  reviewed by staff. Currently **off** by default.
- **"Try your luck" card-matching game:** a popup on every page. "Every player wins a prize." Players give an
  email (with an optional marketing opt-in), and it's one play per person per round. The launch prizes are
  RM5 off (most common), 10% off, or 15% off (rarest). Prize codes are single-use, can't be combined, and
  don't earn points. It's switched on separately by staff.
- **Planned later:** referrals, birthday points, subscriptions (subscription orders may earn bonus points).
  Don't promote these until they exist.
