# Inno Asia Distribution brand kit

Healthcare-focused pharmaceutical and nutraceutical distributor. It supplies clinically supported formulations
to clinics, hospitals and healthcare professionals, and is the company behind Nael & Newk, Collawell and
Phytosure. Machine-readable version (used by `brand.py`): [`brand.json`](brand.json).

> **Still developing:** this kit records the brand as it is today. It's a guide to the feel, not a strict
> rulebook. New ideas, moods and styles are welcome. Only the product facts, no medical claims and no
> generated logos or packaging text are firm.

Source: [innoasia.com.my](https://www.innoasia.com.my/) (collected October 2026).

## Logo

- `logo/innoasia-logo-horizontal.png`: transparent PNG, berry fan mark with the name set to the right.
- `logo/innoasia-logo-stacked.jpg`: the fan mark above the name, on white.
- The fan or shell mark is made of radiating petals in berry `#940848`. Keep it berry on light backgrounds.
- Sub-brand logos are in `sub-brands/` (Collawell is red, Phytosure is blue). These are low-resolution web
  copies, so ask for originals before using them in print.

## Imagery

See `reference/`. There are two families:
- **Science renders**: one translucent glass object (a capsule with molecules inside, a molecule sphere, a
  DNA helix rising from a book, a balance scale with a heart) on a white reflective surface, with a soft blue glow.
- **People**: multi-ethnic Malaysian clinicians in white coats, including women in hijab, working with
  tablets and holographic data in bright modern clinics and labs.

Keep it high-key, white and ice-blue, with lots of empty space. Avoid warm orange casts, gloom, syringes
and blood.

## Voice

- Professional, evidence-based and trustworthy. Written for healthcare professionals and partners.
- Use "clinically supported", "research-driven", "quality-assured", "integrity".
- Be precise and measured. Make no claims beyond what is registered.
