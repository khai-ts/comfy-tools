# Initial brand kit research

- Date researched: 2026-10-12
- Brand: both
- Asked by: khai-ts (setting up this repo)

## Key findings
- Nael & Newk's website uses a warm cocoa palette (`#5A4848`) on white with cream accents, set in Inter. The
  logo is a black rounded lowercase wordmark with green leaves. The tone is calm and plain.
  ([naelnewk.com](https://www.naelnewk.com/))
- Products: Magnesium Bisglycinate 750mg (RM90), Magnesium & Zinc Bisglycinate (RM90), Vitamin D3 + K2 with K2VITAL
  (RM85). Halal (JAKIM) and KKM registered.
- TikTok @naelnewkmy ("Nael&Newk.HQ"): about 12.9K followers and 15.2K likes on 2026-10-12. Bio: "Helping Malaysians
  make smarter wellness choices", run by the team behind Collawell HQ.
  ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@naelnewkmy))
- The membership page content loads with JavaScript. The program's details came from the internal
  `khai-ts/nael-newk-membership` app instead (not launched as of this date).
- Inno Asia Distribution Sdn Bhd (1169950-H): a berry `#940848` fan logo, the tagline "Driven by Science.
  Dedicated to Health.", and clinical white and ice-blue imagery. Brands include Collawell and Phytosure.
  ([innoasia.com.my](https://www.innoasia.com.my/))
- The Inno Asia Wix site still shows template placeholders: a San Francisco address, 123-456-7890, and social
  links to Wix accounts.

## What it means for us
- There are two distinct looks: Nael & Newk is warm and homely, and Inno Asia is clinical and glassy. These are
  captured in `brand/*/BRAND.md` and `brand.json`.
- Marketplace (Shopee) creative is much louder than the website. It's kept as a separate register.
- Open questions: Inno Asia's official font, a white Nael & Newk logo for dark backgrounds, and high-resolution
  sub-brand logos.
